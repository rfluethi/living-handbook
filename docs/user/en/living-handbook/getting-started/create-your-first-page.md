# Create your first page

This guide creates your first handbook page. The steps: write, assign to a handbook, classify, set review data, publish.

<details>
<summary>Requirements: What must already exist</summary>

* A handbook, see [Create your first handbook](create-your-first-handbook.md).
* A user account that may write pages, for example with the role Author, Editor or Administrator. With the role Contributor you write a draft, and someone from the editors then has to publish it. You can edit and view your own page even before it belongs to a handbook.

</details>

## Steps

1. Open **Handbook pages → Add new handbook page**. The familiar block editor opens, first with a choice of patterns, one per page type: Guide, Process description, Tool overview, Role / Organisation, Background / Concept, FAQ and Area overview.
2. Pick the pattern that fits your page, or close the choice for an empty page. A pattern brings only the outline: headings, folded sections and empty paragraphs whose grey placeholder says what goes there. Whatever you leave empty stays invisible on the website. The pattern does not set the page type; you pick that in step 5.
3. Write the page: a clear title, a short opening sentence, then the content.
4. **Assign the page to a handbook in the sidebar.** Almost everyone misses this step. A page without a handbook stays invisible on the website.
5. **Classify the page**, also in the sidebar. Pick the page type (for example Guide or Background), a topic, the audience and the responsible role. These produce the badges on the page and the filters on the handbook's entry page. You manage your own terms centrally, see [Classification and roles](../upkeep/tags-and-roles.md).
6. Fill in the **Handbook maintenance** box at the bottom of the editor: responsible role, last review, review interval. Rough values are better than none. You can refine them later. What these fields do is explained in [The review cycle](../upkeep/the-review-cycle.md).
7. Click **Publish**.

![The editor with the sidebar open, highlighting the handbook assignment and the classification; below it the "Handbook maintenance" box.](../assets/editor-seitenleiste.webp)

## Result

The page is reachable on the website at `/handbook/<page-name>/`. On the entry page of its handbook it appears under "Recently updated". At the top it shows the badges, on the right the "On this page" list. At the very bottom sit the page details, with the review status as a badge.

![The four review-status badges: Reviewed, Review due, Review overdue, Not reviewed](../assets/pruefstatus-abzeichen.webp)

<details>
<summary>Pitfalls: "My page does not show up"</summary>

Almost always it is one of three causes: The page has no handbook assigned. Or the handbook is not visible to the current visitor. Or the page is still a draft. The order to check them in, and the fixes, are under [Frequently asked questions](../faq.md).

</details>
