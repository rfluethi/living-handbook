# Interface

This area explains what your readers see, and above all how much of it you control. Handbook pages are built from blocks and follow adjustable templates. Through the blocks and their settings you set a page's function, which building parts it shows and what it can do; through the templates and a few lines of CSS you set its look. You adjust both without changing the plugin itself. This area shows the three kinds of handbook pages, how the handbooks get into your site's menu, and how you adjust building parts and colours.

| Page | What you can do afterwards |
|---|---|
| [The three surfaces](the-three-surfaces.md) | Tell the overview, the entry page and the single page apart, and adjust each one deliberately. |
| [The blocks](the-blocks.md) | Know the building parts and use them yourself, first of all the Mermaid diagram. |
| [Add handbooks to your menu](add-to-the-menu.md) | Offer the handbooks in the header or in the theme menu. |
| [Customize the design](customize-the-design.md) | Change colours and spacing without touching the plugin. |
