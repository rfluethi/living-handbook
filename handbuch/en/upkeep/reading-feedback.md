# Reading feedback

Below every page is the question "Was this helpful?" with Yes and No. Logged-in people vote, one vote per person and page.

## Where the numbers land

The page list in the admin has a feedback column. It can be sorted by the difference between Yes and No. That is the fastest way to the pages that need work: pages with many No votes rise to the top.

## What you do with it

A page with a poor ratio is not a reason for shame, it is a signal. Read it asking where the confusion comes from, and rewrite the part that causes it.
